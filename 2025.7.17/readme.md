With your team, decide on the genre you want to work with. Complete the Genre Research section of the assignment by analyzing the tropes shared among three games of your genre, and deciding on a twist you will introduce to the genre as part of your paired prototype.
Come up with a rough sketch for the idea of your game and its mechanics. Fill out the Logline, Prototype Description, Mechanic Matrices, and Diagram/Sketch sections of your descriptive document.
